The z-score (-30.0 SD) is reported for scale only and ASSUMES the null's tail is Gaussian that far out -- 500 draws sample the center of the distribution well but cannot verify tail shape 30 SD out; treat the z-score as illustrative, the p-value ceiling as the actual claim. **This rules out pure structural redundancy** as the source of the #3/#4 overlap — if the correlation were explained only by predictor/target overlap, it would be higher than what's observed, not lower. Why the real value sits below the floor is not directly tested here and is reported as an open interpretation, not a settled mechanism.

## Disclosed limitations

- **own_e_b / own_e_r are a reproduction, not a replication** — same data, same estimator, different code. Agreement with published values validates the code; it is not independent evidence for any biological claim.
- **Permutation p-values apply to own_e_b / own_e_r**, transferring to the published metrics only as far as the two agree (rho ≈ 0.975 / 0.983).
- **Residual mid-range disagreement** between own and published e_b is unexplained. It is not the omitted bias correction (agreement is best at the 0/1 edges, not worst) and not the w.post branch threshold (zero variants differ).
- **The A222V reference line** enters every variant's expectation, so its error is common-mode. It shifts the e_b distribution's location but not rank order; all tests here are rank-based.
- **Metric dependence is unresolved.** Rank-based and calibrated central error disagree in sign, and the disagreement survives both the fitness control and the full multivariable model.
