LIMITATIONS: see docstring.  CONSTRUCTIONAL OVERLAP: mean_abs_delta_b and rho_b are both functions of the SAME delta_b, so this is not an independent control -- it removes the overall-magnitude component of delta_b and nothing else, and a surviving D_site_resid is not evidence that delta_b's influence is gone.  mean|delta| is scale-only and blind to direction, so a null here would not rule out a shift confound operating through heteroscedasticity.  The permutation is an association null, not a re-derivation null.  CIs are background-level; the 96 rho_b are mutually correlated and that is not modelled.  Neither task is a decision rule and no outcome word is computed.
